# Project Brief — TrackFlow

## What the company is

TrackFlow is a last-mile delivery and warehouse management company founded in 2009, operating warehouses in Los Angeles and Zaragoza (~130 employees, ~€9M annual revenue). It handles the entire logistics operation for e-commerce brands — storing inventory, picking and packing orders, shipping through a carrier network, and processing returns — so that getting products to customers' doors is TrackFlow's problem, not the brand's.

## What we're building and why

TrackFlow Tech built a candidate management tool (the Talent Pipeline Tracker) to replace an Executive Assistant hiring process that was being run out of a shared Excel file. That file had become unmanageable — too many people editing it at once, no version control, and interview notes getting overwritten. The tool lets the hiring team see all candidates at a glance (name, position, status, stage), filter and search without reloading the page, open a candidate to update their status or stage, add and delete notes, and register candidates who come in through other channels. This solved the disorganization and lost data that the shared spreadsheet caused.

## Project objectives

- Give TrackFlow's hiring team a single, reliable place to manage candidates, replacing the shared Excel file.

- Let the team view, filter, and search candidates in real time, and update status or stage without losing data.

- Safely record and manage notes and candidate details throughout the hiring process.
